Direction A · Coast
Honnavar Food

Rationale. Coast takes today's brand orange and settles it into the colour of a Mangalore-tile roof (#B8461A), on a coconut-cream page with tamarind-brown ink and one Sharavathi-teal accent kept for call, map and delivery cues, so the food photographs carry the warmth. One family, Anek Kannada, sets both scripts — its Kannada and Latin were drawn together, so they match in weight and colour — and its width axis does the hierarchy work: 87.5% for headings and prices, 80% for dense tab and board labels, 100% for reading. Kannada keeps the same pixel size as English with taller line boxes (1.42 headings, 1.62 body) so vowel signs and ottaksharas never clip. Customer screens are generous and photo-led (158 px wide kitchen photos, 18 px card radii, soft layered warm shadows, pill chips); operations screens keep the palette but tighten to a card that scans in one second (number · status icon + word · age / items · total · payment / one action), giving three full partner cards at 390 × 844 and five equal columns at 1280. The known layout problems are solved structurally: one 56 px top bar and one 66 px four-tab bar (15.6% of 360 × 780) where the Cart tab carries the count and ₹ total and the Orders tab carries the live status, so the cart bar and ongoing-order strip disappear; the kitchen header folds name, badges, estimate and call into one card with address and hours collapsed; the cart swaps the tab bar for a single “Place order · ₹total” bar; the partner sound prompt becomes a pill and reject becomes a 2 × 2 sheet that fits one screen; and the board puts assign and reassign on every card.

Motion (all under 200 ms, none under reduced motion)

  • Press feedback — buttons scale to 0.98, chips and tabs tint, 120 ms.
  • Add → stepper — width morphs in place, 160 ms; the Cart tab total counts up, no badge bounce.
  • Suggestions — panel fades and slides 6 px, 140 ms; typing is never blocked.
  • Address and hours — chevron rotates and panel opens, 180 ms, pushing the list (no overlap).
  • Sound prompt → pill — height + fade, 180 ms; remembered on the device.
  • Reject sheet — slides up with scrim fade, 180 ms.
  • Status change — order hero and board cards crossfade, 160 ms; surrounding content does not move.
