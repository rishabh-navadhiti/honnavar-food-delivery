Partner orders

Each card reads left to right in one pass: number, status (icon + word + colour rail), age, then items · item total · payment, then the one next action. The waiting order is the only loud card: turmeric tint, “New” tag, a live countdown and Accept / Reject. Once sounds are on, the prompt shrinks into a pill in the top bar. Rejecting opens a short sheet: four reason chips in a 2 × 2 grid, an optional note and Back / Reject, all inside 390 × 844. Same template in English and Kannada.
