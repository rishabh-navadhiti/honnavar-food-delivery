Honnavar FoodDIRECTION B · LEDGER

Calm, crisp, built for scanning.

Ledger treats Honnavar Food like a well-kept shop register: cool white rows on a pale grey page, near-black ink, and one deep leaf-teal, “Sharavathi” #0B6B57 (6.45:1 with white text), for every action. Turmeric #E8A13A is the only warm accent, kept for things that need attention now: the cart count, the new-order card, the countdown. Type is the Noto superfamily: Noto Sans for Latin and figures and Noto Sans Kannada for Kannada, drawn together so weights and stroke contrast match. Kannada is set at the same size as English with 3–6 px more line height, and every price, total, time and count uses tabular figures so columns line up. Density comes from list rows instead of big cards: 72 px square photos, hairline dividers, 8–12 px radii, and borders instead of shadows (one shadow level, kept for the bottom bar, dropdowns and sheets). That fits more than four kitchens or four dishes on a 390 × 844 screen with no text below 12 px and no target below 48 px. The known layout problems are fixed by structure. There is one 56 px top bar and one 64 px bottom bar (120 px, 15.4% of 360 × 780). The Orders tab carries the live status and the Cart tab the count and ₹ total, so the strip and the cart bar are gone. The kitchen header shrinks to photo, name, badges, ETA and Call, with address and hours in one expander. The cart swaps the tab bar for a single Place-order bar. The partner sound prompt becomes a pill and the reject form a short sheet. Admin moves to a top nav so the five dispatch columns get the full 1232 px. Every order status has its own colour, Lucide icon and word, so colour is never the only signal.

Rules

Kannada is never set smaller than the Latin beside it; it gets more leading through [lang="kn"] tokens. No uppercase or letter-spacing on Kannada (eyebrows turn to sentence case at 13 px). Digits are Latin in both languages (en-IN / kn-IN formatting). The product self-hosts both families; Google Fonts is used only in these mockups.
