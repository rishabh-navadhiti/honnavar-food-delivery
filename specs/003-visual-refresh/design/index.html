003 Visual refresh: three design directions

Static mockups of the six reference screens (home, kitchen, cart, order page, partner orders, admin board) per brief.md and the clarified spec. Phone frames are 390 px with 360 px fit checks; the board is 1280 px. Kitchen and partner orders are shown in English and Kannada in every direction. Photos and names are the real seed catalogue; amounts are illustrative. Pick one, optionally with edits (for example one direction's tab bar in another's style); planning starts after the pick.

A · Coast

Warm and photo-forward: today's orange evolved to Mangalore-tile terracotta on a coconut-cream page, Sharavathi teal only for call, map and delivery cues. One family, Anek Kannada, sets both scripts and uses its width axis for hierarchy. Generous customer screens (wide photo cards, 18 px radii, soft shadows); operations screens tighten to one-glance cards.

B · Ledger

Calm and dense, like a well-kept shop register: white rows on pale grey, one deep leaf-teal for every action, turmeric only for what needs attention now. Noto Sans + Noto Sans Kannada (one superfamily), tabular figures. List rows with 72 px thumbnails, hairlines, 8–12 px radii, nearly flat.

C · Marigold

Bold and icon-led for first-time smartphone users: deep indigo top and bottom bars, marigold only for the single next action (always with ink text), leaf green for good news. Baloo Tamma 2 for names, prices and buttons, Noto Sans / Noto Sans Kannada for body. Chunky 48 px controls, photo beside text, illustrated empty states.
