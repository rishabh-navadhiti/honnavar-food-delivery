Design notes

  • Fixed chrome: top bar 56 px + bottom bar 80 px = 136 px (17.4% of 360×780). The indigo hero with the title and search scrolls away with the page. The dish filter keeps its accessible label “Show dishes” but drops the visible caption.
  • Bottom bar: the Cart tab carries the count badge and the running item total (₹840) in marigold; the Orders tab carries the ongoing order's status with a live dot. Both visible together; no in-page cart bar, no ongoing strip.
  • Suggestions open under the box from the second letter, with the matched letters highlighted. Kitchens first, then dishes with photo, veg mark, kitchen name and price. A kitchen row opens the kitchen; a dish row opens its kitchen scrolled to that dish. The last row runs the existing search. With no match the panel shows one line: “Nothing found for “…””. Opens with a 150 ms fade + 4 px rise; none under reduced motion.
  • Kitchen cards: photo beside text, two compact rows (type and pure veg; open state and delivery estimate) so three cards show per 390×844 screen; the landmark moves to the kitchen page. Closed kitchens keep their place, photo greyed, with a moon icon and the opening time.
  • New strings drafted (not final): tab labels Orders / ಆರ್ಡರ್, Account / ಖಾತೆ; short ETA “35–45 min” / “35–45 ನಿಮಿಷ”.
