Design notes

  • One-second scan: every card reads in the same order: big order number, status chip (icon + text, status colour), the item total under its label (as partners see it today; the delivery fee is not theirs); then time, item count and a payment tag; then the one next step as a full-width button. Waiting orders come first, inverted with an ink outline, a marigold glow and an ink band carrying the countdown.
  • Density: top bar 56 + compact five-tab bar 68 leaves 720 px; the waiting card plus the accepted and preparing cards fit fully at 390×844 in both languages, the ready card starts below. At 360 px in Kannada, wrapped meta lines leave two full cards and most of the third.
  • Sound: first visit shows a full-width marigold prompt (middle frames); after the first tap it shrinks into the green “Sound on” pill beside the title (150 ms height + fade, remembered on the device). If the browser blocks sound again, the pill turns amber with a crossed bell: “Tap for sound”, never the full prompt.
  • Reject is a sheet over the order: four reasons as 2×2 icon tiles, optional note, Back and a red Reject order; all inside 390×844 with no scrolling.
  • Drafted strings (not final): “Sound on” / “ಸದ್ದು ಆನ್”, “Tap for sound” / “ಸದ್ದಿಗೆ ಒತ್ತಿ”, “Cash” / “ಕ್ಯಾಶ್”, “UPI (demo)” / “UPI (ಡೆಮೊ)”, “You will hear a ring for every new order.” / “ಪ್ರತಿ ಹೊಸ ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಬಂದಾಗ ಸದ್ದು ಕೇಳಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.”, tab “Orders” uses the existing “ಆರ್ಡರ್‌ಗಳು”.
