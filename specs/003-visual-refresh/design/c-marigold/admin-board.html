Design notes

  • Top navigation instead of the old left column gives the board the full 1232 px: five equal columns of ~236 px, each with a coloured top rule, group icon and a count pill. The group that needs action (Needs agent) is tinted amber.
  • Cards carry what dispatch needs without opening: number, elapsed time in the current status (red past 20 min while waiting on someone), status chip, kitchen, delivery landmark, total with payment tag, then the agent or an amber “Needs agent” with the assign select and a marigold Assign button on the card. Assigned cards keep a Reassign control.
  • Columns scroll on their own when long; the page never scrolls sideways at 1280 px (and stays usable to 1024 px with columns scrolling sideways, per spec).
  • Finished cards are quieter (off-white, grey number) so live work stands out. Order numbers are Baloo for fast scanning; everything else is Noto Sans at 13–15 px.
