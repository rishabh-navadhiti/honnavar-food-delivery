Design notes

  • One bottom bar: on the cart the tabs give way to the indigo place-order bar with one 56 px marigold button “Place order · ₹870”; the back chevron in the top bar returns to the kitchen. Chrome: 56 + 84 = 140 px.
  • Numbered steps (1 Items · 2 Deliver to · 3 Payment) give first-time users an order to read in; the bill closes the Items card on a tinted, dashed-edge panel so it reads as the receipt.
  • Lines keep the dish photo and veg mark; the stepper and the line total share one row so long names get the full width.
  • Selected address and payment: ink outline, marigold icon tile, ink check disc. The UPI tile keeps a visible “Demo” tag; after paying, the order shows “Paid by UPI (demo)”.
  • “Pick a kitchen and add a dish or two.” is a drafted empty-state line (Kannada draft: “ಒಂದು ಕಿಚನ್ ಆರಿಸಿ, ಒಂದೆರಡು ಐಟಂ ಸೇರಿಸಿ.”), not final.
