Design notes

  • Compact header: a 128 px photo strip with the header card overlapping it; name with a chunky green Call button beside it, type and open badges, closing time, and the delivery estimate on an indigo-tint strip, all in one card. Address and hours are one collapsed row (third frame shows it open; chevron turns in 180 ms, body expands without moving the header).
  • Dish rows: photo first, veg/non-veg mark (Indian square marks: circle = veg, triangle = non-veg) before the name, price in Baloo, a chunky marigold Add that becomes the − n + stepper in place. A dish opened from a search suggestion is scrolled into view and tinted marigold with a left rule, fading after 2 s (fourth frame).
  • Cart tab shows count + running total (₹500 here) instead of an in-page cart bar.
  • Kannada: same sizes as English, line height raised (1.42 headings, 1.68 body). Baloo Tamma 2 sets the kitchen and dish names, Noto Sans Kannada the badges and body; both share a round, open stroke. Conjuncts checked: ಢಾಬಾ, ಸ್ಪೆಷಲ್, ಮಸಾಲ, ಅರ್ಧ, ತಂದೂರಿ.
  • New strings drafted (not final): “Call” / “ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ”, “Address and opening hours” / “ವಿಳಾಸ ಮತ್ತು ತೆರೆದಿರುವ ಸಮಯ”, “4 dishes” / “4 ಐಟಂಗಳು”.
