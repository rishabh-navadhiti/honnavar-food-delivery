Rationale

Marigold is the loud, friendly direction, built for people ordering on a phone for the first time. Deep indigo ink frames every screen with one slim top bar and one bottom bar, so the warm-white page between them holds only content. Marigold #FFB81C is kept for the one thing to do next (Add, Place order, Accept, Assign) and always carries ink text (9.5:1). Leaf green means good news (open, call, delivered), and every order status has its own colour, icon and words. Baloo Tamma 2, one rounded family drawn for Kannada and Latin together, sets names, prices and buttons at the same size in both scripts. Noto Sans and Noto Sans Kannada carry body text, with Kannada given taller lines (1.68 against 1.5), so neither script looks borrowed. Density is medium: 48 px chips and buttons, 16 px card radius, photo-beside-text kitchen cards that show three per screen, and operations screens tightened to show three partner cards and five board columns. The known layout problems are solved by structure. The two header rows, cart bar and ongoing-order strip become one four-tab bottom bar: the Cart tab shows the count and ₹ total, and the Orders tab shows the live status, for 136 px of chrome at 360×780. The cart swaps the tabs for a single Place order bar. The kitchen header is one card, with address and hours folded away. Search suggests kitchens and dishes from the second letter. The partner sound prompt shrinks to a pill, and rejecting an order fits in one sheet. Admin navigation moves to the top, so the five dispatch columns get the full width.

Palette

Contrast is measured against the surface each colour sits on. Marigold never appears as text or as a thin icon on a light ground (1.7:1); it is always a fill with ink on top. Meaning colours always come with an icon, a shape or words.

Type

Baloo Tamma 2 (500–800) for headings, names, prices and buttons; Noto Sans and Noto Sans Kannada (400–700) for body, meta and inputs. Both scripts use the same pixel size at every step; only line height changes (Kannada headings 1.42, body 1.68). Kannada is never set smaller than the Latin beside it. Digits are Latin in both languages.

Motion and checks

  • Press: buttons sink 1 px and lose their lip (120 ms).
  • Add → stepper: cross-fade in place (120 ms); the Cart tab total ticks to the new value and its count badge pops to 1.15× and back (150 ms).
  • Suggestions, expander, sheets: fade + 4–8 px rise (150–180 ms); the expander chevron rotates 180°.
  • Sound pill: the full prompt collapses into the pill (height + fade, 180 ms), with no layout jump below because the cards slide up in the same transition.
  • All motion is off under prefers-reduced-motion; nothing loops except the new-order countdown text.
  • Chrome: top bar 56 + tab bar 80 = 136 px (17.4% of 360×780); cart 56 + place bar 84 = 140 px.
  • 360 px: every phone page was rendered at 360 (#360 on the URL) with an automated check for sideways overflow and clipped text: none.
  • Tap targets: every link and button in the frames measures at least 48×48 (checked automatically).
  • Partner density: three full order cards at 390×844 in English and in Kannada; the reject sheet fits without scrolling.
  • Board: five columns at 1280 px, ~236 px each, no sideways scroll.
  • New interface strings are drafts in both languages and are marked as drafts on each screen's notes.
  • Open point: Noto Sans Kannada looks about 6% smaller than Noto Sans at the same pixel size (see the meta row above). The build could add +1 px to Kannada body and meta (17 / 15) for optical parity; the frames here keep sizes equal, as the rule asks.
